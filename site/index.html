    Where the numbers come from

    An anomaly is how far a year's global average temperature is from the 1951-1980 average. It says nothing about the absolute temperature of any one place. The trend shown above is a straight line fitted to the selected years by least squares, so it summarizes the direction of change but not every wiggle. Seasonal values for the earliest year can be missing because they need December of the year before.
